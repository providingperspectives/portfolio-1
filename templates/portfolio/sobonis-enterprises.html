Sobonis Enterprise Platform

Sobonis Enterprise Nig is a research-led technology platform built to help organizations evolve through software engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud and DevOps, data engineering, cybersecurity, and digital transformation.

Product surface

Home, company, services, industries, research, portfolio, and authenticated member experiences.

Architecture

Feature-driven organization with domain-oriented modules and a reusable design system.

Engineering focus

Scalable, maintainable, accessible, and performance-first delivery using modern web tooling.

Technology system

The platform uses Next.js and TypeScript for the application layer, Tailwind CSS for its visual language, Framer Motion for purposeful interaction, and Supabase-backed workflows for authentication and member features.
